How to Play?

I think of a secret 4-digit number with no repeated digits.

You guess it, and after every guess I tell you:

Bulls: right digit in the right place

Cows: right digit in the wrong place

Eg. Secret 8341, Guess 8032
= 1 Bull (8) and 1 Cow (3)

Crack the code in as few guesses as you can!
